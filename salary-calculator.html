How this estimate works

The tool subtracts your entered percentage from gross pay, then subtracts other deductions. It does not calculate actual statutory tax.

For accurate payroll results, use an official tax calculator for your country and tax year. Currency conversion is not included.

What is take-home pay?

Take-home pay, also called net pay, is the amount remaining after deductions are removed from gross earnings. Depending on the country and employment arrangement, deductions may include income tax, social insurance, pension contributions, and benefits.

How to use this salary calculator

Enter your gross pay and choose whether it is weekly, monthly, or yearly. Enter an estimated deduction percentage and any additional deductions for the same pay period. The result converts the estimated net amount into monthly and annual equivalents.

Important limitation

Tax systems vary by country, income level, residency, and tax year. This generic tool is not a tax calculator and should not be used to file a return or make payroll decisions.
