Loan details

Estimate only

The calculation assumes a fixed annual rate and regular monthly payments. It excludes fees, insurance, taxes, variable rates, and lender-specific terms.

How monthly loan payments are calculated

For a standard amortizing loan, each payment covers interest for the period and repays part of the principal. The calculator converts the annual rate to a monthly rate and uses the number of monthly payments in the selected term.

Before taking a loan

Compare the annual percentage rate, fees, repayment schedule, and total cost disclosed by lenders. This estimate is educational and is not a loan offer or financial advice.
