Adult BMI categories

Common adult categories are under 18.5 (underweight range), 18.5–24.9 (healthy-weight range), 25–29.9 (overweight range), and 30 or above (obesity range).

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not distinguish muscle from fat and may not suit children, pregnancy, or every population group.

What is BMI?

Body mass index is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It is commonly used as a population-level screening measure, but it cannot directly measure body fat or determine an individual's health status.

How to interpret your result

For adults, BMI ranges can provide an initial reference point. Age, muscle mass, body composition, pregnancy, and other factors can affect how useful the result is. A healthcare professional can interpret it alongside other measurements and health history.

Limitations

This calculator is educational and not medical advice. Do not use BMI alone to diagnose a condition or set a treatment plan.
